# Pricing clause for the Terms of Service

The 3D print calculator prices every order from figures the customer's own
browser calculates and submits. The relay sanity-checks those figures, but it
cannot independently verify that a submitted price matches the model actually
uploaded — doing so would require re-measuring the STL server-side (see
"Deferred" in the session notes).

The practical control is that Arcane Flame reviews every order by hand before
printing. For that control to be enforceable, the right to re-quote or cancel
needs to be in the published terms, not only in the calculator's interface.

As of 2026-08-19 the Terms of Service page says only:

> All prices listed on our website are in £ (GBP) and include applicable taxes
> unless otherwise stated.

There is no clause covering estimates, confirmation, or cancellation.

## Suggested wording

Paste into **Online Store → Pages → "Terms of service and Health & Safety"**,
under the existing pricing paragraph.

---

**Custom 3D printing quotes**

Prices produced by our 3D print calculator are estimates, generated
automatically from the file details you provide. They are not a binding quote.

We review every order by hand before printing. If the price calculated for an
order does not reflect the model actually supplied — for example because the
file's dimensions, scale, quantity or material differ from what was submitted,
or because of a technical fault or manipulation of the ordering process — we
will contact you with a corrected price. You may accept the corrected price or
cancel for a full refund.

We reserve the right to cancel and refund any order that has been priced
incorrectly, and to decline orders we cannot print safely or to an acceptable
standard. No order is confirmed until we have reviewed your files and told you
so.

---

## Note

This is drafted as a starting point, not legal advice. If your business relies
on it to cancel paid orders, have someone qualified check it against UK
consumer law — particularly around cancellation rights and refunds for
custom-made goods.
